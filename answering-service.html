The money-losing problem

Never miss another service call.

Most electricians don't have a lead problem — they have a missed-call problem. The phone rings while you're on the tools, and you have to choose between doing the work in front of you and answering it. Either way, something loses. The caller who hits your voicemail rarely leaves one; they dial the next name in the search results.

The cruelest part is which calls slip through. A tripped breaker that won't reset at 7pm. A burning smell at an outlet on a Saturday. Half a house with no power on a Sunday morning. These after-hours and weekend emergencies are the highest-value, highest-intent work you do — and they're exactly the calls most likely to go unanswered, because that's when you're not sitting by the phone. Whoever picks up first wins the job. An answering service makes sure that's you.

The difference

Built for electricians, not a generic call center.

A traditional answering service hands your calls to an operator reading a script in a room full of accounts they don't know. They take a message and promise someone will call back — which means the job is still sitting in your inbox, not on your calendar. WattsBooked is different: it knows electrical work, takes real action on the call, and hands you a booked job instead of a sticky note.

  • It knows electrical jobs — panel upgrades, EV chargers, dead outlets, tripping breakers — so it asks the right questions.
  • It flags true emergencies — a burning smell, a sparking panel, no power — and transfers them to a live person.
  • It books straight into your calendar instead of just taking a message.
  • It logs the lead in your CRM with notes, so nothing falls through the cracks.
