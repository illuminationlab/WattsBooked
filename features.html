Conversation AI

Instant answers on every channel.

Conversation AI is a text-based AI agent that lives on your website chat and across every messaging channel — SMS, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp. It answers questions, qualifies leads, books jobs, and escalates to your team when a human touch is needed.

  • Trained on your data — documents, FAQs, service descriptions, and website content via web crawler.
  • Qualifies leads by service interest, timeline, job details, and location — then routes to the right pipeline stage.
  • Suggestive or fully automated — your call, per channel.
  • Unified inbox — every conversation in one place, regardless of channel.
